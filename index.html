Blackmailing Format - Inside Full Guide (Educational)

Goal: To help you RECOGNIZE and AVOID sextortion scams.

⚠️ What is it? Scammer builds quick trust, requests private intimate images, then threatens to share them with your family, followers, workplace unless you pay. This is a crime.

How It Works - Red Flags:

  • Quick Trust: Stranger says "hello how are you" and quickly asks where you are from, age, hobbies.
  • Shifting Private: Moves to intimate topics and asks to exchange private images.
  • Request: Asks you to send intimate pictures, claims they will send theirs too.
  • Threat: Threatens to distribute to followers, tags, family, work, school. Says they screenshotted everything.
  • Money Demand: Demands $200-$500 via Zelle, Apple Pay, Venmo, gift cards. Says "pay now and I delete and show proof. If you block, I ruin your life."

Common Threat Lines:

  • "I will send to all your followers and family if you don't comply"
  • "If you block me, that will be worst part, I ruin your life"
  • "I sent to my journalist / influencer / your school"
  • "Just pay and I delete and we are done forever"
✅ What To Do If Targeted:
1. Stop communication - do not argue or pay
2. Do NOT send more images or money
3. Save evidence - screenshots of threats
4. Block and report account
5. Tell trusted adult / support service
6. Report to platform and EFCC / FBI IC3
7. Make followers private, warn family you are being targeted

Protection:

  • Never share intimate images with strangers.
  • New account, few posts = red flag.
  • Keep follower list private.
  • Paying does NOT delete - they will ask for more.

This is for education to AVOID scams.
